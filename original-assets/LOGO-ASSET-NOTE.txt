Brand mark note
---------------
This public student-project edition uses a custom typographic "LPU" project mark.
It is not the official Lovely Professional University seal or logo.

LPU's official media page states that its logo is copyrighted and cautions against
unauthorised use. If you have written permission or an approved official logo asset,
place the authorised file in an assets folder and update the brand mark in index.html.
Do not assume that a logo visible on a public webpage is free to reuse.
