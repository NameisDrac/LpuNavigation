          Fresher help desk

          How do I find Block 34?

          Type “Block 34” (or just “34”) in the search box and choose it. The pin is a map reference point supplied by the project owner — confirm the entrance with campus signs.

          How does the walking route work?

          Pick a destination, then press “Start navigation”. The app asks your browser for your location, sends your position and the destination to a public OpenStreetMap routing server, and draws the walking path it returns. If routing is unavailable it tells you, and can show a clearly labelled straight-line distance instead.

          Can I trust every route?

          No. Routes depend on OpenStreetMap data and a community routing service. Indoor routes, campus-only shortcuts and temporary closures are not known. Check signs and follow university access rules.

          Why does my location look wrong or jump around?

          GPS can be weak indoors, between tall buildings or on a laptop. The map shows an accuracy circle and warns you when the signal is poor. Step outside or near a window and wait a few seconds.

          Is my location stored or shared?

          Your position stays in your browser. It is only sent to the routing server when you start a route, and only after you confirm. This site has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies.

          What if a building is missing or a pin is wrong?

          Many numbered blocks are listed but do not have verified coordinates yet. Use the “Report a wrong or missing place” form below, which prepares an email to the project owner.

          Does it work offline?

          If you installed the app or visited before, the page and the place list open offline. Map tiles, routes and live OpenStreetMap data need internet.

          Does this replace UMS or official help desks?

          No. This is an independent student project, not an official LPU service. Use official channels for timetable, attendance, hostel, fee and other administrative matters.

          Privacy details

          • No cookies, accounts, analytics or advertising.
          • Theme, fresher mode, favourites and recent places are saved in your browser’s localStorage. Clear your browser data to remove them.
          • Your GPS position is read by your browser only after you allow it. It is shown on the map on your device.
          • When you start a route, your position (or the campus reference point) and the destination coordinates are sent to routing.openstreetmap.de, a public community service. They are not sent anywhere else by this site.
          • Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap (or Esri for satellite), which receive your IP address and the map area you view. Optional live map data is requested from Overpass servers.
          • The “Prepare email” form opens your own email app; the site itself sends nothing.
